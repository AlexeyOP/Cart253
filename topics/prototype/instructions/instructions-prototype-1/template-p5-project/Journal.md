I also remember that atmosphere of fun and interest! I remember how we were all involved in that construction process and how we had a demonstrative show for other students during the Christmas time.

Making the prototype taught me how ideas fail or succed and improve under working on it.

What surprised me is that I could manage a simple prototype of the robot. And now I can improve the image and add details and add stronger fitures. 

What is cool? I think I can do something better and it is very interesting to me. I will try beginning from a simple prototype to a more complex design.

Though it is a rough computer model it can inspire me and for my future work I will be ready more and more, so I will improve.

And also it is a careful guessing of how to do this correctly.
